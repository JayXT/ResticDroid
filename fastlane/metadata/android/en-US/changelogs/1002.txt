First release.

* Backup profiles: paths, excludes, tags, retention.
* Schedules: manual, every N hours, or daily.
* Conditions: charging, unmetered, idle, battery, Wi-Fi SSID.
* Repositories on B2, S3, Azure, GCS, Swift, REST and local folders.
* Snapshot browsing, file listing, diff, forget, prune, restore.
* Fingerprint or repository-password confirmation.
* Plain-text config in internal storage, watched for edits.
* Bundles restic 0.19.1, built from source.
